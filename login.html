Login

skapi.login() signs the user in with their email address and password. The form's action is the page Skapi opens after a successful login.

Don't have an account? | Forgot password?

skapi.anonymousLogin() signs the visitor in without an account of their own. It creates a temporary account with no email address and no password, which is deleted 30 minutes after the visitor leaves, or at once on logout. A guest who logs in or signs up afterwards leaves the temporary account behind. Anonymous Login is on for projects created at www.skapi.com; the project owner can turn it off in the project settings.
