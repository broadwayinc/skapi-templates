Newsletter

Visitors can subscribe to your newsletter without an account. skapi.subscribeNewsletter() takes the email address and the newsletter group, and sends a confirmation email: the address is on the list once the link in it is clicked. The group public is the newsletter for everyone.

A logged in user subscribes with their account's email address instead, which must be verified, and gets no confirmation email. A guest account cannot subscribe.

To send a newsletter, write it in your own mail app and send it from your project's email address to the list's sending address, shown on the Newsletters page of your project at www.skapi.com.
